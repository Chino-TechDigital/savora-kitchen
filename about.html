our story

From passion to plate

Founded by Savora Okoro, Savora-Kitchen was born from a deep love for food and hospitality. What started as a small kitchen has grown into a beloved dining destination in Makurdi.

We believe every meal should be memorable. That's why we source the freshest ingredients, experiment with bold flavors, and serve each dish with warmth and pride.

“Food is our language of love. Every plate we serve tells a story of heritage, creativity, and care.”
